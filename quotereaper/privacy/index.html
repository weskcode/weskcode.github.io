Privacy policy

Privacy Policy Last updated: September 2026 Quote Reaper ("the App") is an unofficial fan companion app for the Red Rising book series by Pierce Brown. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data. Data Collection Quote Reaper does not collect any personal data. All quotes are bundled app content stored locally on your device. Third-Party Services The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google's ad SDK may collect: - Device identifiers (IDFA/GAID) for ad serving - Product interaction data for analytics - Advertising data for personalized ads This data is collected by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Quote Reaper does not transmit, store, or have access to this data. User Consent Before the Google Mobile Ads SDK initializes, the App presents a consent dialog required by EU/UK privacy regulations (GDPR/UK GDPR). No ad-related data is collected until you provide consent. In-App Purchases The App offers a one-time Ad-Free purchase ($0.99) via Apple's StoreKit 2. This transaction is handled entirely by Apple. Quote Reaper does not transmit, store, or have access to any payment information or receipts. Data Storage All app data (favorites, game statistics, preferences) is stored locally on your device using: - UserDefaults for preferences and game state - App Group (group.com.quotereaper) for sharing data between the main app and home-screen widgets No data is transmitted to external servers. Children's Privacy The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the Last updated date above.
