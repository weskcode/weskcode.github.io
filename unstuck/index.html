Unstuck

A small iPhone app for the task you keep putting off. Type what you're avoiding, how much time you have, and why it feels stuck. Unstuck names the blocker behind it and gives you one first step, with a two-minute timer to get you started. If now isn't the time, you can plan when you'll start. Free, with no account, ads or analytics.
