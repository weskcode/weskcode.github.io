Privacy policy

Privacy Policy for Unstuck Last updated: November 2, 2026 Unstuck ("the app") is developed by Wesley Keetch ("I," "me," "my"). THE SHORT VERSION Unstuck doesn't collect any data. Everything you type stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, and the app makes no network connections. WHAT DATA THE APP TOUCHES, AND WHERE IT STAYS The app saves the following in Apple's on-device storage (UserDefaults). None of it is ever sent to me or to anyone else. - Your most recent result: the task you typed, the reasons you picked, the time you said you had, when you decoded it, and any plan or outcome note you added. The app keeps it so you can come back to your result later, and uses its date to notice when you return to the same task. - App settings: your appearance choice (System, Light or Dark) and whether haptic feedback is on. Only the latest result is kept. Each new result replaces the one before. Like other app data, this is included in your own iCloud or computer backups if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and your device, not by me. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION To delete your saved result, open Settings in Unstuck and tap Clear Saved Result. Deleting the app removes everything it saved. I have nothing to delete on my side, because I never receive any of it. WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO - It doesn't require or create an account. - It doesn't connect to the internet or to any server. - It doesn't include analytics, advertising or tracking, and never reads your advertising identifier (IDFA). - It doesn't use App Tracking Transparency, because there is nothing to track. - It doesn't offer in-app purchases. - It doesn't send notifications. - It doesn't share, sell, or rent any data. - It doesn't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. If you email me for support, you're sharing whatever you put in that email, the same as any email. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY The app isn't directed specifically at children. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app doesn't collect information at all. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change too. Any future change to the app's data practices will be reflected here before it ships, not after.
